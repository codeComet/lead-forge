On the Korenlei since 1998

The kitchen

One family, two generations, the same eleven tables

Margot's parents opened Maison Lorel on the Korenlei in 1998 with a blackboard and a gas ring. She took the kitchen in 2016 after nine years in Brussels and Copenhagen, kept the blackboard, and put in the counter so guests could see what was happening.

Most of what reaches the table comes from within eighty kilometres — the market at Sint-Michiels twice a week, boats at Zeebrugge, and four growers we've bought from for over a decade.

Questions

Asked on the phone most days

I have an allergy. How careful can you be?

Careful, and honest about the limits. Put it in the booking and the kitchen sees it before you arrive; the maître d' will also go through it with you at the table. Because the kitchen is one room and handles all fourteen listed allergens, we can build you a dish that avoids yours but we won't claim a plate is trace-free. If your allergy is anaphylactic, tell us on the phone and we'll talk it through properly before you commit.

Do you cook for vegans and vegetarians?

Every day, and not as an afterthought. There are vegan dishes in each course on the menu, and the whole six-course tasting menu exists in vegetarian and vegan versions at the same price — those need two days' notice so we can order for it.

Is service included? Should I tip?

Service and VAT are both in the prices you see, and the team is paid a full wage rather than a share of tips. Nothing more is expected. People often round up anyway and it's split across the whole house, kitchen included.

Can we bring children?

Gladly, at lunch and early evening. There's no separate children's menu — the kitchen does a smaller portion of anything on the main one for €14. High chairs, a changing table and somewhere to park a pushchair are all here.

What if we need to cancel?

Tell us up to twenty-four hours ahead and there's nothing to pay. Tables of six or more and the cellar room need a card to hold the date, and we charge €35 a head only for a no-show or a same-day cancellation. Private bookings cancel free up to fourteen days before.

Do you take walk-ins?

The six seats at the bar are kept back for exactly that, every service. The counter and the tables are almost always booked two to three weeks ahead, longer for a Saturday.

Is the restaurant accessible?

The dining room, terrace and WC are all step-free from the street. The cellar room is down one flight with no lift — say so when you enquire and we'll put a private group in the main room instead.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, for an amount or for a named menu, sent by email the same day or posted on card if you'd rather hand it over. They're valid two years, which is the legal minimum here, and we honour them after that anyway.
