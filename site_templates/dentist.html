Questions

Asked at reception most days

I'm frightened of the dentist. What can you do about that?

Quite a lot. We book a longer slot so nothing is rushed, agree a hand signal that stops everything immediately, and numb the gum with gel before any injection so you don't feel the needle. If that isn't enough, we can arrange sedation for longer appointments. The first visit can also be nothing but a conversation — no instruments at all.

Do you take public insurance?

Yes. Examinations, X-rays, standard fillings, extractions and children's treatment are billed directly to ÖGK, BVAEB or SVS — just bring your e-card. Ceramic work, implants, aligners and whitening are private, and you'll see the exact figure in writing before we start.

My tooth hurts today. Can I be seen?

Call the practice as early in the day as you can. We keep slots free each morning for pain, swelling and broken teeth, and we'll fit you in the same day whenever it's at all possible.

Is a crown really finished in one visit?

For most teeth, yes. We scan the tooth, design the crown on screen while you have a coffee, and mill it from a ceramic block here in the practice. Around two hours from start to finish, with no temporary crown and no second appointment.

How often should I come for a check-up?

Once a year for most adults, twice if you've had gum trouble, smoke, or wear aligners. We'll tell you which applies to you and send a reminder when it's due.

Can you fix teeth that were treated somewhere else?

Regularly. Bring any X-rays or treatment plans you have and we'll give you an honest second opinion, including when the answer is that nothing needs doing.

What if I need to cancel?

Let us know at least 24 hours ahead and there's no charge — the slot goes to someone waiting in pain. Phone, email and WhatsApp all work.

Toothache right now?

Call before 10:00 and we'll do everything we can to see you today. Swelling, a knocked-out tooth or a lost crown counts as urgent — say so when you ring.
