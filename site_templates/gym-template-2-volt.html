Before you ask

Fair questions.

I've never lifted anything. Is this the wrong place?

It's the right place. Start with Sled & Carry or Barbell Club — both assume you've never touched a barbell, and both are capped so a coach sees every rep. About half of the people in your first class will have joined this year.

What actually happens in the free week?

You get the full floor for seven days plus any three classes and one hour with a coach. No card, no direct debit, and nobody rings you afterwards unless you ask us to.

Is it really that loud?

Yes, on the main floor. If you'd rather not, the mezzanine runs quiet from 09:00 to 15:00 and Reset is always low volume.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Thirty days' notice by email, no phone call, no retention script. Annual members can switch to monthly at any point instead of cancelling outright.

Where do I park?

There's metered street parking on Ritterstraße and a car park on Alte Jakobstraße two minutes away. Realistically, take the U8 to Moritzplatz — it's a 300-metre walk.

Do you have showers and towels?

Twelve showers, towels included in every tier, and a drying room for cycling kit. Bring a padlock or buy one at the desk for €4.
