Why members stay

Three things we won't trade away.

Never more than six

Group sessions are capped at six. If a class fills, it fills — we don't squeeze in a seventh person and we don't add a second row behind the rack.

A programme, not a playlist

After your movement screen a coach writes twelve weeks with your name on it — loads, sets, progressions. You can read it, question it, and take it with you if you leave.

Leave on 30 days' notice

No twelve-month lock-in, no joining fee, no cancellation phone tree. Write to us and the membership ends at the close of the following month.

Six ways in

Programmes, not classes you drop into and forget.

Each one runs in twelve-week blocks with a test week at either end, so you can see on paper whether it worked.

    Your first week

    Four steps, and none of them is a sales meeting.

    Free intro

    Forty minutes with a coach. You walk the hall, say what you want, and ask whatever you like. No card needed.

    We film a squat, a hinge and an overhead reach, then talk through what we saw. Old injuries go on the record here.

    Written within two days and sent to you as a PDF. Twelve weeks, with the reasoning next to each block.

    Before you ask

    The questions we get at the desk.

    I've never lifted a barbell. Will I be the worst one there?

    About a third of our members had never touched a barbell before they joined. Strength Foundations exists for exactly this, and the six-person cap means a coach sees every rep rather than every third one.

    Do I have to sign up for a year?

    No. Memberships run month to month with 30 days' notice, and there is no joining fee. You can also freeze for up to eight weeks a year without paying.

    Can I train without joining a programme?

    Yes — Open gym gives you the full hall, the sauna and the plunge. You just won't have a coach writing for you or a reserved spot in group sessions.

    Is there parking on Refshaleøen?

    There is free parking on Refshalevej directly outside, and covered bike parking for about 120 bikes. Bus 2A stops 200 m away and the harbour bus 991 lands at the quay.

    I'm pregnant, or recently gave birth. Can I still train?

    Yes. Two of our coaches are pre- and postnatal qualified and will adjust your programme each trimester. Bring your midwife's or physio's notes to the intro if you have them.

    What if I book a class and can't make it?

    Cancel in the app up to two hours before and the spot goes back to the waiting list at no cost. After that it counts as used, because someone else could have taken it.
